# TrevioKit requirements

Current specification, including the later payment, reporting and design changes. Replaces SQLite and local-only requirements. Prepare for hosting, but do not deploy without a separate instruction.

1. **Platform:** Next.js App Router, TypeScript, MongoDB Atlas Free (M0), Vercel and Node.js 24.x. MongoDB is the durable business database; use server-side environment variables and reuse connections efficiently.
2. **Inventory:** name, size, selling price, independent stock per name/size, optional HTTPS image URL. Add/edit/search/archive, initial quantity on creation, audited later stock adjustments, total stock row. Add product opens a hidden form; Edit opens stock adjustment. No SKU, categories or cost prices.
3. **Retailers:** name, phone, optional shop/address, archive. Clicking a retailer opens all their invoices, total units sold/returned, total paid and current due/credit. No retailer accounts.
4. **Sales:** required active retailer, multiple unique product lines, integer quantities. Default price from inventory with per-sale override. Deduct stock and create a unique invoice automatically.
5. **Returns/exchanges:** reference the retailer's original purchase, credit original selling prices, restore stock and prevent excess returns. Support sale-only, return-only and combined exchanges. Example: 2 returned at ৳1,000 and 7 taken at ৳1,000 produces ৳5,000 net. Negative net amounts display credit/refund.
6. **Payments:** partial, full or zero payments. Combined payable includes previous recorded due/credit and new net transaction value. A payment can clear old and new due together. Preserve opening balance, total payable, paid amount and closing balance snapshots. Serialize retailer checkouts and reject stale balances. No standalone payment-only entry or refund disbursement tracking.
7. **Statuses:** when current recorded retailer due is zero, earlier recorded invoice table statuses show Fully paid. Otherwise historic due snapshots remain visible. Printed invoice amounts stay unchanged. Legacy invoices without payment records stay Not recorded and are excluded from recorded balances.
8. **Atomicity:** invoices, payment snapshots, stock, return links, history and submission receipts succeed/fail together in a MongoDB transaction. Prevent overselling, excessive returns and duplicate submissions under concurrent requests. Store money as integer poisha.
9. **Invoices:** immutable product/size/price and retailer snapshots, read-only completed transactions, short sequential numbers for new invoices, original invoice references for returns/exchanges. View/Print/Save as PDF in a compact black-and-white A4 layout with retailer shop name. Long invoices continue across A4 pages.
10. **Navigation:** Dashboard, Inventory, New Sale / Exchange, Retailers, Sales / Invoices, Expenses, Analysis. Responsive desktop/mobile Soft Forest workspace colors; centered compact tables; BDT and Asia/Dhaka dates.
11. **Dashboard:** net revenue, total paid, current due, units sold/returned, current stock, interactive trends, recent transactions/activity. All/Today/Last 7 days/Last 30 days/Custom range. Revenue/payment/unit totals follow the range; due and stock are current across all dates.
12. **Sales lists:** retailer and custom-date filters plus search by invoice/retailer/shop. No paid/due filter. Show units, payments and payment status. Complete histories paginate in 100-record batches.
13. **Expenses:** Add expense button opens description, positive amount and expense-date form. Persist record/history/receipt atomically. Read-only history, count and spending total. Dashboard-style date filters use expense dates, including backdated records.
14. **Analysis:** date-filtered revenue, payments, expenses, revenue after expenses, units, sales/expense charts, top 10 products/sizes and retailers by net revenue. Use all matching records; subtract return credits once. Current due/credit uses complete history. Revenue after expenses is not profit because product costs are not tracked.
15. **Access:** one secure owner login, no public registration or employee accounts. Protect every business page/backend operation. Login and framework error pages are publicly accessible. Credentials and secrets stay server-side and outside Git. Optional product images are URLs, with no uploads.
16. **Excluded:** ecommerce, shipping, suppliers, CRM, employee management, fake transactions in real data. Disposable preview/tests use isolated temporary databases.
17. **Delivery:** local startup and tests, placeholder-only .env.example, Atlas setup, backup/restore and Vercel deployment instructions. Vercel Hobby is non-commercial; business use needs an appropriate paid plan. Prepare hosting configuration and leave deployment to a separately authorized step.

Implementation choices: case-insensitive unique product name/size; single original purchase per return/exchange; internal returned counters; retailer settlement version for concurrent checkouts; persistent submission UUIDs; cached MongoDB pool/index setup. Preserve all business collections, including counters and submissions, in full database backups. Restore into an empty isolated destination and invalidate restored sessions before use.
